Grid Garden

Bring every flower into bloom. Each tile changes itself and its four neighbours.

Four small gardens. No time limit.

Moves: 0

Start with a tiny garden and grow from there.

How to play

A blooming tile is green and shows a flower. A sleeping tile is dark and shows a seed. Tap a tile to change it and its neighbours above, below, left and right. Corners change three tiles; centre tiles change five. Make every tile bloom.

Click or tap a tile. On a keyboard, use Tab to enter the garden, arrow keys to move, and Space or Enter to plant. Undo reverses your last move. Hint points to a useful move. You can reset as often as you like. New garden grows a fresh 4×4 garden every time; Original gardens brings back the four classics. Your progress is saved in this browser and resumes when you return.
